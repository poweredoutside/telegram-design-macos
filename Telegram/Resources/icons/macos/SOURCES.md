# Native Telegram macOS assets

Source: TelegramMessenger/telegram-macos, revision `273b7c4870b0ddf1bf3a56afb3e6937109e574ba`.
The donor's GPL license applies to these assets.

For the monochrome icons, the original 1x and 2x alpha channels are stored as RGB masks without resizing. The 3x masks are resampled from 2x with Lanczos. The outgoing call mask only exists at 2x in the donor: its 1x and 3x masks are resampled with Lanczos. PNG row order is preserved for rendering in the Qt canvas. Colored settings icons retain their original 2x RGBA pixels and render at 24 logical pixels. The Premium mask renders at 16 logical pixels inside the 24-pixel gradient tile defined by `generatePremiumIcon()` in the donor's `packages/TelegramTheme/Sources/TelegramTheme/Appearance.swift`. The four tab animations retain the original compressed TGS bytes and play in a 48-pixel canvas as defined by `TabAnimatedIconView` in `packages/ChatListUI/Sources/ChatListUI/TabBadgeItem.swift`.

Regenerate or verify with `tools/macos_design/sync_assets.py --donor PATH` and the optional `--check` flag.
